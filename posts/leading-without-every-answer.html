Leading Without Having Every Answer

Apr 28, 2026 · 5 min read

Leadership is not a performance of certainty. A team usually needs direction, honesty, and a way to make progress while information is still incomplete.

I trust leaders who can say what they know, what they do not know yet, and what decision they are making anyway. That kind of clarity gives people room to contribute instead of waiting for perfect confidence to arrive.
