How I Think About Technical Debt

Apr 9, 2026 · 7 min read

Technical debt becomes dangerous when it is unnamed. Once the team can see it, price it, and connect it to product risk, it becomes part of normal decision-making.

Not every rough edge deserves immediate cleanup. The question is whether the debt is slowing current work, hiding failure modes, or making future change more expensive than the team realizes.
